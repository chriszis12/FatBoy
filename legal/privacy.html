Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 October 2026. App: FatBoy ("the app"). Developer: Spaceland Studios (Christos Zisopoulos), Greece. Contact: spacelandstudios@protonmail.com.

  • Your profile, weights, meals, workouts, progress photos and settings are stored on this device. Progress photos never leave it.
  • Accounts are optional. If you sign in with Google or email, a copy of your progress and settings is stored with our backend provider, Supabase, so it can sync between your devices.
  • If you join Social, your username, profile, posts, comments, messages, group activity and the stats you share (XP, streak, badges) are stored on Supabase and shown to others as your privacy settings allow. Direct messages are not end-to-end encrypted.
  • When you ask for an AI recipe, the ingredients and notes you type are sent to Google's Gemini to write it. Nothing else is sent.
  • Food search and barcode scans send your search words or the barcode number to Open Food Facts. The scanner is Google's code scanner, and FatBoy does not keep camera images.
  • Reminders are scheduled on your phone. FatBoy has no ads and no analytics or tracking, and it does not sell your data.
  • Your choices: export a backup in Settings, sign out at any time, delete your account in Settings to remove your synced data, Social profile and posts, or reset all data to wipe this device.
  • Reports and moderation records are kept only as long as needed to keep the community safe.
  • Data controller and contact: Spaceland Studios (Christos Zisopoulos), spacelandstudios@protonmail.com. You can ask us to access, correct or delete your data at that address.
  • Data is processed by Supabase (database, sign-in and storage) and, when you use the recipe assistant, by Google (Gemini).
