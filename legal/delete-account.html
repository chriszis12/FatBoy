Delete your FatBoy account and data

Last updated: 24 October 2026. App: FatBoy ("the app"). Developer: Spaceland Studios (Christos Zisopoulos), Greece. Contact: spacelandstudios@protonmail.com.

  • In the app: Profile, Settings, Account, then Delete account. This removes your synced progress, Social profile, posts and messages.
  • Cannot open the app? Email spacelandstudios@protonmail.com from the address you signed up with and we will delete the account.
  • Data stored only on your phone (meals, weights, photos) is removed when you use Settings, Reset all data, or uninstall the app.
