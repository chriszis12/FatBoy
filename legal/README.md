# Legal pages

These are plain-language drafts built from the in-app text. They are **not legal advice**.
Before you publish the app:

1. Replace every `[PLACEHOLDER]` (name, country, email, date).
2. Have a lawyer in your country read them. Health and diet apps carry extra risk, and rules differ by country (GDPR in the EU/Greece, state laws in the US).
3. Host the three pages anywhere public (GitHub Pages is free) and paste the links into the Play Console: Privacy Policy URL and the account deletion URL (`delete-account.html`).
4. Fill in the Play Console Data safety form to match the privacy page (account email, user content, app activity, photos stay on device).
5. Consider business insurance and registering as a company before you take money for the app.
